About Me

Computer Science is my passion. Clarke's law says "Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic", That fanciful appeal of computer science is what hooked me into becoming a computer science major.

Ever since I was young, I was enthralled with, what I believe to be humanities greatest achievement, space travel. It isn't know by many, but thousands of inventions that benefit humanity every single day came from spaceflight. Things like GPS, CT machines, compact electronics and even diapers came from it. I study computer science because it's what I believe the future of space exploration depends on.

A big topic in the computer science world, and quite a controversial one at that, is artificial intelligence. My view of AI is simple. I wan't to become as capable as I can as a programmer before resorting to AI. AI is a good tool for speeding up production of code, but it deteriorates the programmers abilities. The whole point of computer science is in the name, science. Science is meant to extend humanities knowledge. However, as things are currently, AI cannot push the boundaries of its own knowledge like humans can.
